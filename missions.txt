FULL 150 MISSIONS (30 × 5)

1 Social greetings & introductions
1. Find out the person’s full name  
2. Find out where the person is from  
3. Find out what the person does (job/study)  
4. Find out one thing they like to do  
5. Get the person’s phone number  

2 Casual conversations with friends
1. Find out what your friend did yesterday  
2. Find out your friend’s favorite food  
3. Find out if your friend is free this weekend  
4. Find out your friend’s favorite movie or series  
5. Get your friend to agree to meet tomorrow  

3 Family gatherings & discussions
1. Tell your mom about your day  
2. Find out what your brother/sister wants to eat  
3. Ask grandma how she makes her special dish  
4. Find out when the family is meeting next  
5. Get your dad to watch a movie with you tonight  

4 Dating & romantic interactions
1. Find out if the person is single  
2. Find out the person’s favorite café  
3. Get the person to go for coffee this week  
4. Find out what kind of music they like  
5. Get the person’s phone number  

5 Professional networking & meetings
1. Find out what the person’s job is  
2. Find out which company they work for  
3. Find out one thing you have in common  
4. Get their LinkedIn or email  
5. Get them to agree to have coffee soon  

6 Job interviews & career advice
1. Tell the interviewer why you want the job  
2. Describe your best skill with an example  
3. Ask about the salary  
4. Ask when they will decide  
5. Get them to say you are a strong candidate  

7 Workplace collaborations & feedback
1. Ask your colleague to help you with something  
2. Offer to help your colleague  
3. Ask your boss for feedback  
4. Ask for one day off next week  
5. Get your teammate to agree with your idea  

8 Academic discussions & lectures
1. Ask the teacher to explain something again  
2. Ask when the next exam is  
3. Ask a classmate for the homework  
4. Ask the teacher for more time on a project  
5. Tell the teacher you liked the lesson  

9 Everyday shopping & transactions
1. Find out the price of something you want to buy  
2. Ask if they have it in a different color/size  
3. Get a small discount  
4. Return something you bought yesterday  
5. Buy two things and pay  

10 Dining out & restaurant interactions
1. Order food and a drink  
2. Ask if something has nuts or meat  
3. Ask for the waiter’s recommendation  
4. Complain about something (cold food/slow service)  
5. Ask for the bill and pay  

11 Travel planning & bookings
1. Book a train or bus ticket  
2. Find out the price of a flight  
3. Change a booking to a different day  
4. Ask for a window or aisle seat  
5. Cancel a ticket  

12 Airport & transportation logistics
1. Check in for your flight  
2. Find out the gate number  
3. Ask why the flight is delayed  
4. Report a lost bag  
5. Ask for a meal voucher  

13 Asking for & giving directions
1. Ask how to get to the train station  
2. Ask how to get to a bank or pharmacy  
3. Ask if something is close/far  
4. Ask where the bathroom is  
5. Ask how to get to the city center  

14 Hotel & accommodation arrangements
1. Check in to the hotel  
2. Ask for a quiet or better room  
3. Complain about something in the room  
4. Ask for late check-out  
5. Check out and pay  

15 Medical consultations & health advice
1. Describe your symptoms to the doctor  
2. Ask if you need medicine  
3. Ask for a sick note  
4. Tell the doctor about an allergy  
5. Ask how long you will be sick  

16 Emergency situations & help requests
1. Say you lost your wallet/passport  
2. Ask someone to call the police/ambulance  
3. Say your phone was stolen  
4. Say you feel very bad and need a doctor  
5. Ask for help finding the hospital  

17 Banking & financial discussions
1. Open a new bank account  
2. Withdraw money  
3. Ask why your card doesn’t work  
4. Change dollars/euros  
5. Ask for a new card  

18 Legal consultations & advice
1. Explain your problem to the lawyer  
2. Ask how much it costs  
3. Ask what documents you need  
4. Make an appointment  
5. Ask how long it will take

19 Community events & volunteering
1. Ask how to volunteer  
2. Find out the next event date  
3. Sign up to help  
4. Ask what you will do  
5. Invite the person to volunteer together  

20 Sports & fitness activities
1. Book a class or court  
2. Ask about prices or schedule  
3. Invite the person to play sport  
4. Ask for beginner tips  
5. Join a gym  

21 Hobbies & leisure pursuits
1. Find out the person’s favorite hobby  
2. Tell them about your hobby  
3. Invite them to do something together  
4. Ask if they play an instrument  
5. Ask them to show/teach you something  

22 Cultural events & arts appreciation
1. Buy a ticket to an event  
2. Ask about discounts  
3. Find out start time  
4. Ask the person’s opinion about art/music  
5. Invite them to a museum/concert  

23 Media & entertainment reviews
1. Recommend a movie/series  
2. Ask their favorite show  
3. Convince them to watch something  
4. Find out if they like a genre  
5. Say why you liked/disliked something  

24 News & current events debates
1. Tell today’s big news  
2. Ask their opinion  
3. Find out where they read news  
4. Share one good news story  
5. Agree or politely disagree  

25 Politics & social issues discussions
1. Ask who they voted for  
2. Ask their opinion on one topic  
3. Say one thing you want to change  
4. Ask why they think that  
5. Find out their view on climate change  

26 Environmental & sustainability talks
1. Ask how they help the environment  
2. Convince them to use less plastic  
3. Share three easy green tips  
4. Ask if they recycle  
5. Invite them to a clean-up  

27 Technology & gadget troubleshooting
1. Explain your phone problem  
2. Ask how to fix Wi-Fi  
3. Complain your device is slow  
4. Ask for the price of a new one  
5. Get free repair or accessory  

28 Customer service & complaints
1. Complain about late delivery  
2. Return something online  
3. Cancel a subscription  
4. Complain about bad internet  
5. Ask for money back or compensation  

29 Negotiations & bargaining
1. Get 20–30% off something  
2. Buy and get a free gift  
3. Sell your old phone  
4. Get free delivery  
5. Negotiate a lower price on furniture  

30 Farewells & reflective conversations
1. Find out where your friend is moving  
2. Get them to promise to stay in touch  
3. Invite them to visit you later  
4. Find out what they will miss  
5. Say a proper goodbye
